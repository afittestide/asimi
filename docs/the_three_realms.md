## The Three Realms — 三界

A realm is **whom a thing lives under**, not what it is made of.
One filesystem; three jurisdictions. A citizen is **transfigured** at the
border, never merely carried — hence *ascension*, not migration.

Keep three axes apart or every ruling contradicts the last:
  - **staging state** — where a change legally stands (not territory)
  - **territory** — which ground a citizen occupies (not legal state)
  - **realm** — which law governs the thing

### heaven 天 — the observed sky and the crowned ledger

  - Not the filesystem; a set of sources and a method for reading them.
  - **Authoritative, not infallible.** Green CI is testimony, not reality.
  - **evidence is not verdict** — the runner banks testimony; only the
    Judge's pen files. Keep the pen and the bench in different hands.
  - **the sky** — testimony, live, largely rented (logs, OTel, metrics).
    Legitimate *if declared*. Thin: citation coordinates + times.
    **Cite, don't clip** — context is not severable into a verdict.
  - **firmaments** (durable verdicts) — judge/ → precedents/ → seals/
  - **heaven.conf** — instrument decree: sources, **trust**, citation
    rule, retention, rendering for a reader of another script.
  - **seed + URL** — every verdict carries an irreducible seed (verdict,
    commit, source id, time range, window hash) *and* the URL. The seed
    is ground; the URL may rot. A verdict may cite a dead star and stand.
  - **two registers, one heaven** — 史冊 (seals/, immutable) and
    現行 (in force, mutable: production, the living sky).
  - **ascension ritual** — Judge's Seal (sound) → Chancellor's Seal
    (form) → Ruler's Seal (will) → 頒行 (promulgation → 現行).
    The seal crowns the record; promulgation enlivens the sky.
  - The court's *memory* is fixed; the sky it lives under is alive.
    Durability is not permanence — never name this realm "absolute."

### earth 地 — one filesystem, three jurisdictions

  - **Capital** — committed, unpushed.  **Middle Kingdom** — staged.
    **Borderlands** — unstaged. Harmony: wu-wei flow between them.
  - **民 citizen** — a file.  **邑 settlement** — a directory
    (self-similar: 邑 may contain 邑, to any depth; cities are born of
    the citizens who live in them — no 邑 without a 民).
  - **州 province** — a worktree: a branch seated on its own ground.
  - **支 branch** — a line of history, a pointer to a commit; no ground
    of its own until seated as a 州.  **宗** — the ancestral line.
    **歸宗** — merge (returning home).
  - **輩** — a generation: the siblings born of one parent commit.
    **宗族** — a clan: the set an edict's line has touched.

### Ren 人 — the living will

  - The Ruler's desires, requirements, clarifications — *living* intent.
  - The sovereign reads another tongue: **legibility is the deliverable.**
  - **omission markers** (TODO/FIXME/XXX/HACK) are Ren petrified into
    Earth — will frozen at authoring, undated, unowned, unverified.
    They are **short-lived**. The ascension ritual sweeps the 宗族 under
    review (at the Chancellor's seal) and each must be **handled** (made
    a ling/edict, with a durable id) or **removed** (struck with a ruling
    that says why). There is no holding pen. Removal is never silent.

### the court

  The court is not *in* any realm — it is the **threshold**, the ray
  crossing between them. That is why it appears on no realm's map, and
  why its whole office is **faithful rendering across tongues**.

### The Seal Chain — ascension

  Judge's Seal → Chancellor's Seal → Ruler's Seal → 頒行.
  `seals/` is the only immutable history. Only Edict 1 does not ascend.
