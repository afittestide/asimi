# The Ruler's Guide

🎂 *For `vi`'s fiftieth birthday — the tool that will live forever.* 🎂

Welcome. In Asimi, **you are the Ruler** (君主). The AI ministers work for you:
you speak intent, they execute. This book is your guide — from installation to
running a small government over your own project.

This guide is for **users** who have installed Asimi via brew, the one-liner
installer, or a binary. If you want to work on Asimi itself, see the
[README](https://github.com/afittestide/asimi) → Development.

## What Asimi is

Asimi is an opinionated, multi-model coding agent with an unusual interface:
a **government**. You don't prompt an unopinionated agent — you rule a small court
of specialist ministers, each with their own domain, their own tools, and their
own limits. They translate your intent into working code, and you hold the seal
that authorizes it.

This is not a costume. The structure is lifted from the *three departments and
six ministries* (三省六部) of imperial China — a division of labour that ran an
empire for a millennium. Its principles turn out to be exactly what a careful
coding agent needs: separated powers, named roles, written rulings, and a ruler
who is allowed to be wrong.

If you have never heard of the Tang dynasty or its bureaucracy, do not worry —
that is precisely why **Part I** exists. By the end of it you will understand
what the court *is*, why it is shaped this way, and why a government beats an
unopinionated agent for serious work. Only then do we install the tool.

## How to read this book

Read it in order the first time. The ideas build on one another, starting with
the metaphor itself.

1. **The Court** — what Asimi is, the three departments, the three realms, the
   seal chain, and the ministers. *Start here, even if you're eager.*
2. **Getting Started** — install Asimi and issue your first edict.
3. **The Machinery** — edicts, lings, rituals, and zhengming in depth.
4. **The TUI** — tabs, modes, and the vi keys you already know.
5. **Working with Models** — providers, login, and model selection.
6. **Configuration** — config files, sessions, context, the sandbox.
7. **Reference** — troubleshooting, and the philosophy underneath.

> **Prefer the terminal?** Much of the command and keybinding detail is also
> available offline with the built-in help — try `:help edicts`,
> `:help modes`, or `:help rituals` inside Asimi.

Ready? Turn the page.
