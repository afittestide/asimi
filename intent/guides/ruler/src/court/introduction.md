# The Court

Before you install anything or run a single command, you must understand *what*
Asimi is. This chapter builds the mental model the rest of the book depends on:
why Asimi is shaped like a government rather than an unopinionated agent, where the
idea comes from, and how its parts fit together.

Read it in order. Its five sections build on one another.

1. **[What Is Asimi?](what-is-asimi.md)** — why a court, not an unopinionated agent.
2. **[The Three Departments and Six Ministries](three-departments.md)** — the
   thousand-year-old system Asimi borrows from.
3. **[The Three Realms (三界)](three-realms.md)** — the jurisdictions in which
   everything lives: 人, 地, 天.
4. **[The Seal Chain](seal-chain.md)** — how a change ascends from proposal to law.
5. **[The Ministers](ministers.md)** — who serves the court, and what each is
   forbidden to do.
